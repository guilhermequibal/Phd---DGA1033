### 6.3 Single-credit and scoring queries

- **Single-credit:** return the credit's applicability determination first, then (if Applicable/TBD) its evaluation entry and sources.
- **Scoring / award:** return `assessment_overview` + `category_breakdown` + sources, always reflecting the post-applicability denominator.
